📚 Bài Tập 6 – Đọc Hiểu

Đọc 3 đoạn văn của Jeanne, Laura và Thea, sau đó trả lời câu hỏi bằng cách chọn tên người phù hợp.

Jeanne

At school, we played a lot of team sports, including basketball, volleyball, and football. But I'm not very fast or strong, so I couldn't hit or kick a ball hard. My teammates often laughed at me, so I stopped playing sports. But later I learned that you can get exercise without other people around. Now I sometimes go cycling, swimming and running, but I usually do things alone. Then no-one can get mad at me when I'm slow!

Laura

Back when I was in school, I joined many sports. And I liked being a member of a team. We shared some wonderful moments. We traveled to matches by bus, and had parties when we won. Then I was in a terrible car accident three years ago, and I hurt my legs. It still isn't better. It's made me really unhappy. Going fishing is the only sport I can do, and it's so boring! But one of my friends gave me advice on sailing. It sounds fun, so I think I'll try that.

Thea

I don't hate team sports, but I prefer doing sports when there aren't lots of other people making noise. That's why I like climbing. It's just me and the mountain and a few other people who also like quiet, beautiful places. I think I would enjoy skiing too, for the same reason, but it is expensive and only in winter.

💬 Bài 4 – Hoàn Thành Hội Thoại
Đọc đoạn hội thoại và chọn đáp án đúng để điền vào chỗ trống.
✏️ Bài 5 – Thay Thế Cụm Từ In Đậm
Đọc hội thoại bên dưới, thay thế các cụm từ in đậm bằng từ/cụm từ đã học trong bài.

Sally: Hi, David. I made you a cup of coffee. Just how you like it, milk and two sugars.

David: Oh, thanks, Sally.

Sally: You're welcome. So what do you do when you're not working?

David: Well, I'd like to (1) go out and meet my friends, or (2) ride my skateboard in the theme park near my house.

Sally: I especially prefer to (3) watch movies at the cinema.

Sally: When I am alone, I like to (4) learn about new things through book's pages or (5) enjoy the music of my Kpop boy band.

📖 Bài 6 – Đọc Hiểu
Đọc đoạn văn (có thể cuộn để xem hết) rồi trả lời câu hỏi bên dưới.
Hello!

My name is Nora and I live in Sweden. I live in a small village in the mountains with my parents and my little sister. She is six and I am eleven.

My mum's name is Helen and my dad's name is Nick. My mum is a fitness coach and has to go to the gym everyday. My dad is a farmer and he has a small milk farm. That's why I love both sports and animals.

Everyday, I get up at 6.30 and do morning exercises together with my mum. We have a big breakfast and our mum takes my sister and me to school by her car. After lessons I stay at school to play basketball. It's my favorite sport. At that time my sister goes dancing at the school club. After that I go home with my sister by bus because my mum has some evening classes.

After school I like to help my dad to take care of cows. They are so nice! In the evening I like reading books or hanging out with friends. We are interested in going cycling on the weekend. I have got a cool green bike. I think I am the best bike rider because I go in for sports every day.

📊 2. Danh từ vs Tính từ — Noun vs Adjective

Danh từ (Noun)Tính từ (Adjective)Nghĩa
📌 Quy tắc: Danh từ đứng sau động từ hoặc đứng một mình.
Tính từ đứng sau động từ "be" hoặc trước danh từ.
Ví dụ: "There is wind." (danh từ) ↔ "It is windy." (tính từ)
💡 Hậu tố tính từ thường gặp:
wind → windy  |  cloud → cloudy  |  fog → foggy
snow → snowy  |  sun → sunny  |  storm → stormy  |  ice → icy

📖 Bài 6: Đọc hiểu – Warren Faidley

Warren Faidley has an unusual job - he likes bad weather so much that he follows storms, really bad storms like tornadoes. He was born in the middle of the USA. This part of the world has more tornadoes than the rest of the country. Warren travels all over North America to find tornadoes and other storms. When he finds one, he makes a film of it and uses his computer to give him more information. He says he enjoys taking photos of bad weather. He believes it is very important for people to know about bad weather. He works for the government and also for Hollywood. He helped on the film Twister. He says his job can be very frightening but he does everything he needs to do to keep himself safe.
